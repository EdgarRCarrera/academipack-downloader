# Legal and Responsible Use

This project is intended for authorized access only. It describes technical boundaries and responsible-use expectations for the repository. It does not certify that any particular use is lawful, compliant or authorized.

## Core restrictions

- Use only your own account and your own active session
- Do not share or reuse third-party credentials, cookies or tokens
- Do not bypass authentication or authorization
- Do not bypass paywalls, DRM or other technological protection measures
- Do not discover hidden resources, guess endpoints, inspect hidden LMS APIs or enumerate identifiers
- Do not send excessive or disruptive requests
- Do not extract personal data, exams, assignments, grades, forum content or student submissions
- Do not redistribute downloaded materials without authorization
- Personal study use does not automatically authorize redistribution
- Institutional conditions may be more restrictive than this repository's documentation
- Follow the terms, policies and instructions that apply to your institution and jurisdiction

## Important limits

- Laws vary by jurisdiction and by the facts of each situation
- This document is not legal advice
- A disclaimer does not legalize prohibited conduct
- Technical safeguards in this repository do not replace the need for authorization

## Official legal sources often consulted

### France

- Code penal, articles 323-1, 323-2, 323-3 and 323-3-1: [Legifrance](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/section_lc/LEGITEXT000006070719/LEGISCTA000006117598/)
- Code de la propriete intellectuelle, article L122-5: [Legifrance](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000048603495)

These official texts are commonly consulted for unauthorized access to automated systems, interference with those systems, tooling designed for such offenses, and limited copyright exceptions. They do not create a general permission to copy, redistribute or bypass restrictions.

### Mexico

- Codigo Penal Federal, including the official text of the 211 bis series and related unauthorized-access provisions: [Camara de Diputados PDF](https://www.diputados.gob.mx/LeyesBiblio/pdf/CPF.pdf)
- Ley Federal del Derecho de Autor, including the official text governing limited reproduction exceptions and technological protection measures: [Camara de Diputados PDF](https://www.diputados.gob.mx/LeyesBiblio/pdf/LFDA.pdf)

These official texts are commonly consulted for unauthorized access or copying of protected information, copyright limits and technological protection measures. They should be reviewed against the specific facts of the situation.

## Practical meaning for this repository

- The tool is meant to automate downloads that the user can already perform manually from visible LMS links
- The tool must not be used with someone else's account or session
- The tool must not be used to revive access after enrollment or authorization has ended
- The tool must not be used to discover endpoints, hidden files or resources not presented in the LMS interface
- Downloading materials for personal study does not automatically authorize redistribution, public posting or resale
